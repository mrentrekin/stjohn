First visit

What to expect

✦
Do I need to be Orthodox to attend?

No. Everyone is welcome at every service. You're welcome to stand, sit, or simply watch and pray. Nobody expects you to know what to do.

What language are the services in?

[Parish to confirm: the mix of Greek and English used in the Liturgy.] Service books with translations are available.

How long is the Divine Liturgy?

The Divine Liturgy runs about 90 minutes and is preceded by Orthros at 8:45 AM. You're welcome to arrive at any point.

What should I wear?

Modest, respectful clothing, the kind you'd wear to any special occasion. There's no strict dress code.

Can I receive Holy Communion?

Holy Communion is offered to Orthodox Christians who have prepared to receive it. Everyone is invited to come forward at the end of the Liturgy to receive the antidoron, blessed bread.
