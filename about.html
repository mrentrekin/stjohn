The restoration

Heaven on earth, restored

Our church recently went through an extensive restoration, and the work included new Byzantine iconography. In Orthodox tradition, icons are "windows to heaven." The color, light, and images of the saints surround the faithful and draw them into the worship of the whole Church, on earth and in heaven.

Clergy

Our presiding priest

Father Nicholas Samaras

Presiding Priest

Fr. Samaras's family comes from Gargalianos and Tripoli, Greece. His grandparents emigrated to Woburn, Massachusetts. He graduated from Hellenic College and Holy Cross School of Theology, studied at the Aristotelian University of Thessaloniki, and lived on the island of Patmos. He holds a Master of Theology, a Master of Fine Arts from Columbia University, and a Ph.D. from the University of Denver.

He was ordained with the blessing of Saint Paisios and has served for 32 years: in the Diocese of Denver, in Grand Junction, Colorado, in Tarpon Springs and Lecanto, Florida, and for sixteen years in West Nyack, New York. He loves to focus on faith education, spiritual growth, and serving the people. He is married to Presvytera Evyenia Diacovasilis Samaras, and they have two children, Anastasia and Kallistos.

The Orthodox Church honors St. John as the Prodromos, the Forerunner, who prepared the way for Christ and baptized Him in the Jordan.
