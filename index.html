A word of welcome

Come and see

Our parish has weathered many changes over the years, and through them all it has kept its faith and kept growing.

Whether you grew up in the Orthodox Church or are stepping inside for the first time, you'll find the same Divine Liturgy that has been prayed for centuries, beautiful iconography, and a parish family glad to meet you. Stay after Liturgy for coffee hour. We'd love to hear your story.

— Fr. Nicholas Samaras, Presiding Priest
